## Font-generated assets

`scripts/make-assets.ps1` generates individual glyph sprites using installed Impact and Georgia fonts. No standalone font binary was found in this repository. However, absence of a font binary does not establish permission to distribute a bitmap font. [Microsoft's font redistribution FAQ](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/typography/fonts/font-faq) distinguishes text images from conversion into individual glyph bitmap fonts. Confirm the font source and appropriate redistribution rights, or arrange a separately reviewed replacement, before further distribution.
